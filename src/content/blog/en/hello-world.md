This is the first post.

I set up a blog to document the pitfalls I encounter, interesting things I learn, and occasional life fragments.

Why not use an existing platform? Because something you build yourself is truly yours. A domain + a static site costs a few bucks a year, all data lives in git, and it won't disappear when a platform shuts down.

From now on, writing and publishing is just a `git push`. Simple as that.
